# Security and Responsible Use

TRACE is designed for authorized forensic examination, research, education and incident-response triage. Only collect or analyze evidence you are legally authorized to access.

TRACE does not include mechanisms to bypass authentication, defeat encryption, evade security controls, or covertly access third-party systems.

Do not treat automated findings as proof of identity, intent, culpability or guilt. Preserve original evidence and follow the procedures required by your organization and applicable law.
