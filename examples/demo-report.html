Methodology

TRACE separates observed artifacts from inferred relationships. Relationships are generated by explicit rules and expose their supporting basis and confidence score.

Hashes identify the bytes examined at acquisition/analysis time. A hash match is evidence of byte identity, not identity of a person.

Automated indicators can be incomplete or wrong. Absence of an artifact does not prove absence of an event.
